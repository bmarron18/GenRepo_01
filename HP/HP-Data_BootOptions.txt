Accessing the Boot Menu or BIOS
Power off your computer completely.
Turn it on and tap the Esc key right away to open the Startup Menu.
Press F9 for the Boot Menu to pick your USB drive directly, or press F10 to enter the BIOS Setup.

----------------
Changing Boot Order in BIOS
Go to System Configuration or Storage using the arrow keys.
Select Boot Options.
Find the USB Diskette on Key or USB Hard Drive entry and move it to the top of the boot order list (use F5/F6 or follow on-screen directions).
Turn off 
Secure Boot under the Security tab if your USB drive fails to show up or boot.
Press F10 to Save and Exit, then confirm to restart your computer from the USB.
